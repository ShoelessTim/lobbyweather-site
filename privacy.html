Lobby Weather

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Lobby Weather (“Lobby Weather,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with the Lobby Weather application on Roku devices (the “App”) and related public information at lobbyweather.com.

Lobby Weather is a free weather display for lobbies, waiting rooms, lounges, and personal use. There is no user account and no login.

1. Summary

  • We do not create accounts or ask for passwords.
  • We do not collect payment information in the App.
  • Location preferences stay on your Roku device (device registry), not on a Lobby Weather server.
  • Weather and map data are requested from third-party providers using the place you choose; those providers have their own policies.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • The App does not show third-party advertising at this time.

2. Who we are

The App is offered under the trade name Lobby Weather. Public product information and these legal pages are published at lobbyweather.com.

Contact for privacy and support: support@lobbyweather.com (or the support address shown on the Roku channel listing, if different).

3. Information we process

3.1 Information you provide on the device

You may set a location (for example a US ZIP code or place search) and other display preferences (units, theme, an optional house audio stream, mute). These settings are stored locally on the Roku device using the Roku registry (or similar device storage). We do not operate a Lobby Weather cloud account that stores your settings or location history.

3.2 Weather, maps, and location data sent to third parties

To show forecasts, radar, and maps, the App sends requests over the internet to third-party services. Those requests typically include coordinates, ZIP/place identifiers, or map tile parameters needed to return data. Providers may log IP addresses and technical request metadata under their own policies.

Providers used by the App may include (and may change over time):

  • US National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) — current conditions and forecasts (US)
  • NOAA / NWS radar (opengeo.ncep.noaa.gov) — radar imagery (US)
  • NWS Weather Prediction Center — national surface analysis (fronts and pressure centers)
  • Open-Meteo — forecasts outside the US or when NWS is unavailable, and city/postal-code lookup
  • Zippopotam.us — resolve a US ZIP code to coordinates

We do not control those services. Please review their privacy policies and terms. If a provider is unavailable, parts of the App may fail or show incomplete data.

3.3 House audio (optional)

The App is silent by default and ships no music. An operator may enter the address of an audio stream on their own network (for example an Icecast server). The App then connects only to that address; the setting stays on the device. Lobby Weather does not see or receive that stream.

3.4 Roku platform data

Roku, Inc. and its affiliates operate the Roku OS, Channel Store / Launchpad, and related services. They may collect device, usage, crash, and store analytics under Roku’s privacy policy and terms. That processing is separate from Lobby Weather. We do not run our own analytics backend.

3.5 This website

The public site at lobbyweather.com is static legal and product information. It does not require an account. Hosting providers (for example DNS or static-site hosts) may process standard server logs (IP address, user agent, timestamps) as part of delivering the site. We do not use advertising cookies on these pages.

4. How we use information

Information handled through the App is used only to:

  • Display weather, radar, maps, and related UI for the location and options you choose
  • Remember your on-device preferences
  • Respond to support requests you send us by email
  • Maintain and improve the App (for example fixing bugs you report)

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Location-related parameters are shared with weather and map providers as needed to operate the App (see section 3.2). We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, or the integrity of the service. If we use a contractor solely to operate email or hosting, they may process contact messages on our behalf under appropriate terms.

6. Retention

On-device preferences remain until you change them, uninstall the channel, or reset the device. We do not maintain a Lobby Weather server-side profile of your location. Email you send to support may be retained as needed to respond and for ordinary business records.

7. Security

The App uses HTTPS for network weather and map requests where the platform and provider support it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. You are responsible for physical access to lobby or home devices that run the App.

8. Children

The App is a general-audience weather display and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us by email, contact us and we will delete it where appropriate.

9. Your choices

  • Location: Change or clear the ZIP/place in App settings (or reinstall / factory-reset the device as needed).
  • Audio: Leave house audio unset, or mute sound in settings.
  • Uninstall: Remove the channel from the Roku device to stop local use.
  • Support email: Do not include sensitive personal data you do not want retained when contacting us.

10. International users

The App may be used outside the United States where Roku and data providers are available. Forecast narrative from NWS is US-oriented; other sources may be global. Processing by third parties may occur in various countries under their rules.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the product or laws change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy. Material changes may also be noted on lobbyweather.com or the Roku listing when practical.
